# Xiaohei (the character)

## Definition

Xiaohei (the name means "little black") is the fixed visual IP for these article illustrations — a small, solid-black creature.

Every image should include Xiaohei by default. Xiaohei is **not** a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration — it's a deadpan worker seriously operating inside the system.

## Look

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, sometimes thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular, hand-drawn outline.
- Blank, vacant, calm, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very earnest, doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, never saccharine.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Genuinely responsible for some job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Typical jobs

Let Xiaohei carry the core action:

- Hauling material.
- Pulling strings to converge sources.
- Stuck at a breakpoint.
- Working a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Slicing the "material fish".
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Leading a handoff path by a string.
- Holding a warning sign over a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Beside the scene: carrying bricks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting, logging.

## Don't

- Don't make Xiaohei an over-cute mascot.
- Don't draw it as a children's-cartoon character.
- Don't give it elaborate clothes, emoji faces, or shiny eyes.
- Don't let it just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let it steal the structural expression.
- Don't make it too commercial, too round, too polished.

## The test

If you remove Xiaohei and the core metaphor still fully holds, Xiaohei was too decorative — rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei is the **subject of the action**.
