# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, lots of white space, restrained, quirky, product-sketch feel, handwritten feel, clearly structured but never an instruction manual.

Like an explanatory doodle a longtime AI / product / design / dev-tools person sketched on a blank sheet of paper.

## Must

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm grey, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: the subject occupies ~40–60% of the canvas; keep at least 35% empty; ideally one whole quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten English labels: at most 5–8 of them, each ideally 1–4 words.
- One image says one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express structure naturally; never write the structure-type name on the image.

## Color

- **Black** — main line art, the character, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- **Red** — key callouts, problems, emotional beats, critical reminders, results.
- **Orange** — main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, A→B movement.
- **Blue** — secondary notes, mental/internal state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue isn't required on every image. Keep color restrained — less rather than more.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute cartoon poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI.
- No realistic app screenshot.
- No complex background, gradient, shadow, or texture.
- Don't explain every node.
- No top-left type-title like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap".

## Aesthetic direction

Aim for quirky, creative, interesting, clean, imaginative.

Avoid cute, childish, overly complex, or stiff.
